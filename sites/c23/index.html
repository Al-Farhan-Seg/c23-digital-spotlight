Why C23 Digital Spotlight Exists

Good work is happening in our alumni network, but too much of it is difficult to discover, understand and share.

Many alumni are building businesses, offering professional services and running meaningful community projects. Yet their entire online presence may be scattered across WhatsApp statuses, social-media pages or messages that disappear after a few hours.

C23 Digital Spotlight helps selected alumni and their networks turn what they already do into a clear, credible and easily shareable web presence, so potential customers, employers, collaborators and supporters can understand their work and take the next step.

  1. 01

    Hard to discover

    Potential customers and collaborators may never know that the right person already exists within their alumni network.

  2. 02

    Hard to trust quickly

    Scattered posts and forwarded messages rarely communicate services, experience and contact information as clearly as a focused website.

  3. 03

    Hard to share professionally

    A single website link is easier to remember, revisit and recommend than multiple screenshots, phone messages and social-media profiles.

The initiative is not simply about giving away websites. It is about making alumni skills, businesses and opportunities easier to find, trust and support.

What the winner receives

Every selected participant receives a focused, professional website that can realistically be designed, reviewed and launched within seven days. Larger requirements are not ignored. They are simply handled separately as optional paid upgrades.

Your Free Starter Website

Everything needed to establish a clear, credible and shareable online presence.

  • One custom one-page website
  • Up to six meaningful sections
  • Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts
  • One primary conversion action
  • Social and contact links
  • WhatsApp or enquiry path
  • Basic metadata and social-sharing information
  • One consolidated revision round
  • Launch on a farhansegujja.com subdomain (e.g. maria-cakes.farhansegujja.com)
  • Twelve months of campaign hosting
  • Seven days of post-launch defect correction
