Why C23 Digital Spotlight Exists

Great work exists in our alumni network but too much of it is still hard to find, trust and share.

Many alumni are building businesses, offering professional services and running meaningful community projects. Yet their entire online presence may be scattered across WhatsApp statuses, social-media pages or messages that disappear after a few hours.

C23 Digital Spotlight helps selected alumni and their networks turn what they already do into a clear, credible and easily shareable web presence, so potential customers, employers, collaborators and supporters can understand their work and take the next step.

Why this matters

  1. Hard to discover

    Potential customers and collaborators may never know that the right person already exists within their alumni network.

  2. Hard to trust quickly

    Scattered posts and forwarded messages rarely communicate services, experience and contact information as clearly as a focused website.

  3. Hard to share professionally

    A single website link is easier to remember, revisit and recommend than multiple screenshots, phone messages and social-media profiles.

The initiative is not simply about giving away websites. It is about making alumni skills, businesses and opportunities easier to find, trust and support.

Who qualifies

Eligibility, Readiness and Criteria

Selection is based on verified Class of ’23 membership, readiness to provide content quickly, and a clear weekly rotation—not popularity alone.

Eligibility

A person is eligible if they studied at Kawempe Muslim Secondary School as part of the Class of ’23 at any point between S1 and S6.

  • Completed all six years at the school
  • Studied only from S1 to S4
  • Joined for one year
  • Attended for only one term

Verification required

The phone number entered in the “Class WhatsApp group number” field must be a number currently registered in the Class of ’23 WhatsApp group. Applications that provide a number not found in the group will not be considered.

Readiness

A selected person must provide all required website information within 24 hours.

  • Written business or personal information
  • Approved contact details
  • Images and logo where available
  • Any information needed for a referred person

If the required material is not provided within the 24-hour window, the slot may be passed to another applicant. The original applicant remains in the application pool and may be selected again later. This rule applies whether the selected person needs the website personally or refers another qualified person.

Criteria

One participant will be selected each week during the four-week pilot.

  • Selection alternates between a male and a female participant
  • Applications remain in the pool throughout the pilot
  • Applicants do not need to reapply every week unless their details change
  • The pilot lasts four weeks
  • If the pilot is successful, the initiative may continue as a longer-term campaign
