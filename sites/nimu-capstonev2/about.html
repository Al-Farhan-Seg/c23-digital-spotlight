About · Bwaise Industrial Area, Mambule Road

From the Bwaise yard.

Nimu Timber & Woodworks supplies Musambya and Kalitunsi timber to contractors, builders, carpenters and homeowners for construction, roofing, furniture making and general building work.

What follows is the business’s own description of itself. Anything the owner has not yet confirmed is flagged.

How the yard works

Timber, storage and supply.

As the business describes its own operations. None of this is independently certified.

The timber

Musambya and Kalitunsi, supplied for construction, roofing, furniture making and general building work.

Storage and cutting

Timber is stored inside a warehouse, which the business says dries it within a few days. It can be cut to the size a job needs.

Bulk and delivery

Bulk supply for contractors and large projects, with delivery offered to customers who need timber brought to their site.

Customers

Two kinds of buyer.

The business serves individuals and businesses, and this demo keeps their ordering paths separate.

B2C · Individual customers

Homeowners, small builders and carpenters

Buy single pieces or small quantities for a repair, a piece of furniture or a small building job. Pricing is negotiable and discussed directly on WhatsApp.

See the ordering steps

B2B · Business customers

Contractors and companies

Buy in bulk for construction sites and workshops, with delivery coordinated for larger loads and, in this demo, bank transfer offered as an additional payment route.
