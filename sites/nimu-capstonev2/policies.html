Policies

Educational examples of the policies a timber shop would publish.

These policies are written as coursework examples for BUC3106. They are not a claim that Nimu Timber & Woodworks has adopted them, and no legal compliance review has been carried out.

Delivery

Collection from the Bwaise Industrial Area yard is available at any time during business hours. Where delivery is requested, the business and customer agree a delivery window and cost before the order is confirmed. Larger or bulk orders may take longer to arrange than a single-plank order. This description is illustrative of a typical timber-yard delivery process and should be confirmed against the owner’s actual practice.

Returns and refunds

Timber should be checked against the agreed product code, size and quantity at the point of collection or delivery. Any issue should be raised immediately, before the timber is cut or used. Where the business agrees that an item does not match what was ordered, a replacement or a refund to the original payment method would be offered. Because timber is cut and prepared to order in many cases, requests raised after the timber has been altered may not be eligible for a refund. This is an illustrative example, not a confirmed business policy.

Privacy notice

If this demonstration collected customer information, it would be limited to what is needed to process an order: name, phone number or WhatsApp contact, delivery address, and order details such as product code and quantity. It would be used only to confirm, prepare and deliver an order, and to follow up on a payment or delivery question. It would be accessible only to the people fulfilling the order, not shared with unrelated third parties, and kept only as long as needed to complete the order and handle any related query.

This description is written with reference to Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, as a coursework exercise in what a lawful notice should cover. It is not a claim that this site, or Nimu Timber & Woodworks, has been independently reviewed or certified as compliant with that Act or any other law.

Terms of service

Prices discussed on WhatsApp or by phone are not final until confirmed by the business. Orders are considered placed once a price, quantity and payment method are agreed by both sides. The business reserves the right to decline an order it cannot fulfil, for example if stock is unavailable. These terms are an educational example for BUC3106 and are not in force on the live Nimu Timber & Woodworks business.

Customer communication and security

Order discussions happen over WhatsApp or phone call with the numbers published on this site. Customers should be cautious of any other number claiming to represent Nimu Timber & Woodworks, and should verify a quotation directly with the published WhatsApp contact before paying. The business would never ask a customer to pay a stranger’s personal Mobile Money number for a timber order.
