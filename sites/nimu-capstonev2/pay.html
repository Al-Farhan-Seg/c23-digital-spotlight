The process

Five steps, in order.

  1. Choose a product

    Note the code, timber type and size. Check whether it is marked SAMPLE.

  2. Send an enquiry

    Message on WhatsApp with the code and quantity. Businesses can request a quotation by phone.

  3. Agree price and quantity

    Pricing is negotiable. Confirm collection or delivery.

  4. Pay

    Mobile Money and bank transfer are paid first. Cash on delivery is agreed first and paid at handover.

  5. Verify and confirm

    Prepaid: funds are verified before preparation. Cash on delivery: total and address are confirmed. Confirmation comes before collection or delivery.

Step 5 in detail

Prepaid or on delivery.

Mobile Money and bank transfer

Confirmed only when the money shows in the business’s own account or Mobile Money line. Timber is not prepared or released before.

Warning: never trust a payment screenshot alone. Confirm the funds have arrived before releasing timber.

Cash on delivery

Total and address are agreed first. Payment is made at handover and recorded on a signed delivery note.

After confirmation

Handover and refunds.

  1. Confirmed

    The business confirms the order and a collection or delivery date.

  2. Prepared

    Timber is pulled from stock and cut to size if requested.

  3. Handed over

    Collected from the yard, or delivered and checked against the agreed quantity.

If a confirmed order cannot be fulfilled, the amount paid would be refunded by the same method once both sides agree.
