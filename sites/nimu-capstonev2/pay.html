How to Pay & Order

A sample ordering workflow, start to finish.

This page demonstrates how a timber order could be placed and paid for. This is a student project for BUC3106 at MUBS. Payments and orders are not active. No form here collects real customer data or triggers a real purchase.

Step-by-step sample ordering workflow

  1. 1

    Choose a product

    Browse the Products page and note the product code, timber type, and size you need. Confirm whether the entry is marked SAMPLE.

  2. 2

    Send an enquiry

    Message the business on WhatsApp with the product code and quantity, or, for a business order, request a quotation by phone.

  3. 3

    Agree the price and quantity

    Pricing is negotiable. The business confirms a final price, quantity, and whether the timber will be collected or delivered.

  4. 4

    Pay using a method below

    Choose Mobile Money, bank transfer, or cash on delivery. Card payment is shown as a proposed option only.

  5. 5

    Verify and confirm

    The business verifies the payment before preparing the order. The customer receives confirmation before collection or delivery.

Payment methods

Every sample account, number and reference below is fictitious and created for this coursework demonstration only.

MTN Mobile Money

Illustrative
  1. Dial the Mobile Money menu and select Send Money.
  2. Enter the sample merchant number 0771 00 00 00 and the agreed amount in UGX.
  3. Send the transaction reference to the business on WhatsApp for verification.

Airtel Money

Illustrative
  1. Open the Airtel Money menu and select Send Money.
  2. Enter the sample merchant number 0750 00 00 00 and the agreed amount in UGX.
  3. Send the transaction reference to the business on WhatsApp for verification.

Bank transfer — business customers

Illustrative
  1. Request the sample bank details: account name “Nimu Timber & Woodworks (Demo)”, account number 0000 1234 5678, sample bank “Demo Commercial Bank”.
  2. Transfer the agreed invoice amount and keep the bank-issued receipt.
  3. Email or WhatsApp the receipt and invoice number for verification before delivery is scheduled.

Cash on delivery

Illustrative
  1. Confirm the delivery address and the agreed total with the business before dispatch.
  2. Pay the delivery driver in cash and request a signed delivery note.
  3. Keep the delivery note as proof of payment and delivery.

Card payment

Proposed, not active

Card payment is included here only as a proposed future option for discussion in class. No card payment gateway is connected to this site, and no card details should ever be entered here.

How payment is verified

In this sample workflow, the business treats a Mobile Money or bank transaction as confirmed only once the money is visible in its own account or Mobile Money line, not from the sender’s message alone.

Warning: never trust a payment screenshot alone. Screenshots can be edited or sent in error. Always confirm the funds have actually arrived before releasing timber or scheduling delivery.

Refunds, in brief

If a confirmed order cannot be fulfilled as agreed — for example the stock is unavailable — the amount paid would be refunded through the same payment method used, once both sides agree on the issue. See the Returns & refunds section on the Policies page for the full illustrative policy.

Delivery and order confirmation

  1. 1

    Order confirmed

    Once payment is verified, the business confirms the order and a collection or delivery date.

  2. 2

    Timber prepared

    Timber is pulled from stock and, if requested, cut to size ahead of collection or delivery.

  3. 3

    Delivered or collected

    The customer collects from the yard, or the order is delivered and checked against the agreed quantity on arrival.
