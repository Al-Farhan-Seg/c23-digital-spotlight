Why customers choose Nimu Timber.

Three reasons builders, carpenters and homeowners come back — based on what the business already tells customers on WhatsApp and in the yard.

01

Timber that is kept dry

Stock is stored inside the warehouse, so it is ready to work with in a few days rather than left to season outdoors.

02

Cut to your size

Musambya and Kalitunsi timber can be cut to the lengths a specific job needs, instead of selling only fixed stock sizes.

03

Supply that scales with the job

From a single homeowner repair to a bulk order for a contractor, the yard supplies both small and large projects.

Built for two kinds of customer.

The catalogue and the ordering flow on this demo separate individual buyers from business buyers, because they shop differently.

Individual customers

Homeowners & small projects

Single pieces or small quantities of timber for repairs, furniture or small building jobs, paid for by Mobile Money, cash on delivery, or in person.

Business customers

Contractors & companies

Bulk timber for construction sites and workshops, with bank transfer as an additional payment route and delivery coordinated for larger loads.
