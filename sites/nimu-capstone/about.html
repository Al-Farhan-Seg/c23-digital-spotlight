About

A timber supplier built around the Bwaise yard.

Who Nimu Timber & Woodworks serves, what it supplies, and how individual and business customers are treated differently.

Built on dependable supply and lasting relationships.

Nimu Timber & Woodworks is a supplier of high-quality Musambya and Kalitunsi timber, based in the Bwaise Industrial Area on Mambule Road. The business serves contractors, builders, carpenters and homeowners who need timber for construction, roofing, furniture-making and general building work.

Timber is stored inside a warehouse, which the business says keeps it dry within a few days, and it can be cut to a customer’s required size depending on the job. The stated goal is durable timber, competitive and negotiable prices, reliable customer service and timely delivery.

Two kinds of customer, two kinds of order.

The business already serves both individual buyers and businesses; this demo keeps the two paths separate on the How to Pay & Order page.

B2C · Individual customers

Homeowners, small builders and carpenters

Buy single pieces or small quantities for a repair, a piece of furniture or a small building job. Pricing is negotiable and discussed directly on WhatsApp.

B2B · Business customers

Contractors and companies

Buy in bulk for construction sites and workshops, with delivery coordinated for larger loads and, in this demo, bank transfer offered as an additional payment route.

What the business says it values

  • Durability. Musambya and Kalitunsi are described as suitable for demanding construction and furniture use.
  • Fair, negotiable pricing. The business invites customers to discuss pricing directly rather than publishing a fixed price list.
  • Dependable delivery. Delivery is offered to customers who need timber brought to their site.

These are the business’s own stated values, carried over from the live website. No independent verification, review, award or survey is claimed here.
